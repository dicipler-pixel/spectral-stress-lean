# What is not proved here

Lean proves exactly the statements written, under exactly the hypotheses written.

* The Meijer-G Conservative Matrix Field itself, its flatness, and the biorthogonal frames are
  not formalized. The theorems here are the algebra that holds once those objects are given.
* Theorem 1 (the exponents −2, −3, −2), the values of `ρ_c` (Prop. 3), the three two-cycles
  and the location `n* = 0.0603…` of the exceptional point are Richardson-extrapolated
  measurements. The proofs take them as inputs and derive the amplitude and the sum rule exactly
  from them.
* Lemma 1, Proposition 5 (the Petermann decomposition) and Proposition 6 are not formalized.
